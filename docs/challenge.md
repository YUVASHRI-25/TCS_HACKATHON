# Challenge 07 – Theme 2: AI for Business

## Challenge Overview

Financial institutions process millions of transactions daily. Suspicious financial activities are often
distributed across multiple customers, bank accounts, merchants, devices, IP addresses and beneficiaries.

These activities may appear normal individually, making it difficult to identify hidden fraud networks and
complex financial crimes.

## Core Problem

Build an AI-powered Financial Crime Investigator that can connect scattered financial data, discover hidden
relationships, identify suspicious transaction patterns and explain why an entire network may be involved in
financial crime.

## What Our AI Must Do

1. **Entity Resolution:** Identify related or duplicate entities across different records.
2. **Relationship Graph Construction:** Connect customers, accounts, devices, merchants and transactions.
3. **Anomaly Detection:** Identify unusual financial activities and transaction patterns.
4. **Hypothesis Generation:** Generate possible explanations for suspicious network behaviour.
5. **Evidence Gathering:** Collect supporting records for every suspicious finding.
6. **Alternative Explanations:** Consider legitimate reasons behind suspicious relationships.
7. **Confidence Scoring:** Provide explainable confidence and risk assessments.
8. **Investigation Narrative:** Generate detailed reports with recommended next steps.

## Expected Workflow

1. Financial Data Collection
2. Entity & Relationship Discovery
3. Network Graph Construction
4. Anomaly & Pattern Detection
5. AI Investigation & Hypothesis Generation
6. Evidence & Alternative Explanations
7. Risk Assessment & Investigation Report
8. Human Investigator Review

## Proposed Technologies

| Component | Technology |

| Graph Database | Neo4j |

| Machine Learning | Scikit-learn |
| LLM | Claude / GPT / Gemini |

| Graph Visualization | Cytoscape.js |

## Expected Outcome

An intelligent investigation platform that transforms fragmented financial data into connected, explainable
financial crime investigations.

**Key Objective:** Discover suspicious financial networks, explain the evidence behind them, consider
legitimate alternatives and help human investigators make informed decisions without autonomous legal or
punitive decisions.
